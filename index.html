Privacy Policy

Cat Defense: Zombie (package: com.catdefensezombie.game)
Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cat Defense: Zombie ("the game", "we", "our") handles information when you play the game. The game is developed by ducdev.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (for example levels, coins, gems, cats and settings) is saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this progress may be lost.

3. Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through the Unity LevelPlay mediation platform and its ad network partners. These services may collect and use certain information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This is handled by those providers under their own privacy policies. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. In-app purchases

If purchases are available, they are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google handles payment information under the Google Play terms and privacy policy.

5. Network access

The game uses an internet connection to show ads and to process purchases. The game's core gameplay can be played without an account.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the advertising and payment providers described above, to the extent needed for ads and purchases to work.

8. Your choices

You can stop all data collection by the game by uninstalling it. You can limit ad personalization in your device's Android privacy settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, with the "Last updated" date above.
